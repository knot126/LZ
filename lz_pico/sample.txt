
                                Miles of Miles

Tails watched the minutes pass by as he waited, slowly, for Sonic to stop by. He
wasn’t normally the impatient type, but the years of living with the hedgehog
had certianly made him less tolerant to long waits, especially when they involve
Sonic himself, who really ought to be the fastest guy in the world. Given, he
didn’t exactly let Sonic know that he wanted to see him, but he knows he should
be visiting soon.

***

The young kit internally sighed, then refocused on the device in front of him
that he eagerly awaited to show Sonic: a device capable of applying a linear
transformation to any object, which he dubbed the Tails Transformer. With this
device, I could not only teleport objects, but also roate and even scale them!
Just thinking about the the last ability gave the fox a strange, gleeful
feeling, though he admits he wasn’t quite sure why. Before he gives himself
time to properly reason about his feelings, he immediately remembers the thing
annoying him most at the moment.

Maybe I should just test it before Sonic gets here, the kit reasoned internally,
I know he doesn’t like it when I test inventions on myself first, but I think
it’s safe.

With some hesitation, Tails starts thinking about what to do with the
Transformer. The thought of scaling himself up immediately comes to mind. Why
not?, he figures, then I barely have to think about the transformation matrix
I’ll use, and it will be easy to reverse if I mess it up.

I think doubling in size would be a good start. He walked to the middle of his
lab, hoping it would aid his effort not to accidently destroy anything if he
misjuged how large doubling his size would be. He entered the matrix that should
correspond with doubling in size on the device, then aimed it at himself.

As soon as he pressed the Apply button, a glow appeared around his body and at
one tip of the device. He could feel his body expanding. It was working, and …
oh, wow, it felt amazing.

He savoured the feeling of growth: the slight draft of wind as his bangs were
pulled downward, his tails expanding with their huge, fluffy furs dragging
against the ground, the bigger noises he was now able to hear, and the change in
perspective as he grew upwards.. It all made him feel significant. Like a bit of
a god.

The multi-tailed fox wagged his tails in excitement. Woah, that was amazing.
Everything looked so small. He was so big. He immediately wanted to grow more,
though he figured getting so much larger in the house would almost certianly
destroy it, something Tails wasn’t intent on doing right now.

Tails pressed the button to open the hangar leading to the runway he built for
his plane, only to realise he probably didn’t have enough room to walk out of
his hangar while the plane was still there. He sighed, figuring he would just
push the plane out of the way.

Which he did with considerable ease, even easier than it would have been for him
normally, given his now larger size.
